A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

FAQ

A clearer start to your notebook.

What is Trade Notebook?

A personal trading journal with performance statistics, chart reviews, trading plans, risk tools and a separate backtesting space.

Can I log several trades on the same day?

Yes. Batch entry lets you record multiple scalps in one session.

Does it place trades with my broker?

No. You enter trades manually. Broker and platform settings help describe your account; they do not connect to order execution.

Can I review live trades and backtests separately?

Yes. The journal separates live trades from backtests, and the chart library can filter by trade type and pair.

Can I draw on my charts?

Yes. Open a chart to zoom, add lines, text and shapes, or use the mistake roadmap to connect notes and corrections.

Can I change the appearance?

Choose from light and dark themes in Appearance. You can also customize the color of each dashboard statistic.

Are these real trading results?

The numbers and charts on this page are illustrative examples. Your journal shows the trades you record.
